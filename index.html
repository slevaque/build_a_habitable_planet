                      How to use the laboratory

                      Change one planetary input, then observe the planet, evidence strip, status cards, and Mission Control. Extreme worlds are investigations—not failures.

                      Choose Life Lab to interpret that same unchanged planet for six broad forms of life. Category changes ask a new biological question; they do not change the planet or enter Recent Changes.

                      Choose Save Snapshot to preserve a scientific experiment, then open Discoveries to compare two saved planets. Suggested discoveries enter the log only when you explicitly save them.

                      Keyboard directions

                      • Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between controls.
                      • Use arrow keys to adjust sliders and select radio choices.
                      • Use Enter or Space to expand control families and status details.
                      • Mission Control updates when a change is committed, so slider movement does not flood the history.

                      Earth Reference loads the calibration benchmark. It is a reference—not the “correct” planet.
